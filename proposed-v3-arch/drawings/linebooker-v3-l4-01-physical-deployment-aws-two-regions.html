Where Every Running Thing Sits

Where does every running thing sit, from the system down to the availability zone, and what runs beside that placement? Defers: instance types, node counts and sizing (LB-V3-L3-12); cluster shape (sheet 2); cost (sheet 3). This is a directional document: a first-pass position as at 18 September 2026. Nothing on this sheet is built.

SCOPE

One question. Where does every running thing sit? Cluster shape is sheet 2, cost is sheet 3.

DIRECTION

Directional. A first pass as at 2026-09-18 so work can start. Not a decision and not a forecast.

CELL

In V3 the cell boundary and the AWS Region boundary are the same boundary (L4-01-002).

ZONE

An availability zone is one or more data centres with their own power, cooling and physical security. It is not one building (L4-01-005).

ZONE ID

Zones are named by zone ID, afs1-az1, not af-south-1a. The account-local name is shuffled per account; the ID is not (L4-01-006).

OPT-IN

af-south-1 is opt-in. An account must enable it before anything can be placed there. ap-southeast-2 is on by default (L4-01-003, L4-01-004).

THREE

Three NATS servers across three zones is the smallest number that keeps a majority when one zone is lost (L4-01-008).

ONLY NATS

Three NATS servers prove NATS availability only. Every other stateful part states its own replica count and zone spread (L4-01-010).

BESIDE

Placement and runtime are two chains, not one tree. The only join is: this instance runs on that compute in that zone (L4-01-007).

OPEN

Zone spread and failover for Postgres, the object store, Temporal, ingress, nodes and volumes is open. See L4-01-O07, owned by Architecture.

Cluster Shapes T3, T4 and T5

Which clusters exist, who votes, and what does each shape survive? Defers: which shape is chosen (L4-01-O01, open, Architecture); cost per byte, which is sheet 3. This is a directional document: a first-pass position as at 18 September 2026. Nothing on this sheet is built.

SCOPE

One question. Which clusters exist, who votes, what survives? Cost is sheet 3.

DIRECTION

Directional as at 2026-09-18. No V3 decision of record exists (L3-08-O10, owned by Architecture).

AXIS

Cluster is the axis that separates the shapes. Region, tenant, account and cell are the same in all three; the JetStream domain is not (L4-01-009).

GATEWAY

A gateway joins two clusters into one supercluster with one meta group (T3, T4).

LEAFNODE

A leafnode link joins a cluster to a hub and keeps its own meta group and its own domain (T5).

ARBITER

The arbiter holds no stream assets. It votes, and that is all (L4-01-030).

HUB

If T5 is chosen the hub sits beside the za cell in af-south-1. One region failure then takes the hub and the za cell together; the au leaf keeps serving (L4-01-016, L4-01-017).

EVIDENCE

Demo 03 ran these shapes as bare processes on one Mac. It measured NATS quorum, not AWS (L4-01-031).

OPEN

Which shape is chosen is open. It is decided by which failure boundary the business is buying (L4-01-O01, owned by Architecture; L4-01-O03, owned by the business owner).

Failure Boundaries and Transfer Cost

Which failure is being bought, and what does each shape cost per byte moved? Defers: the actual GB per day per cell (L4-01-O04, open) — every figure here is a shape, not a forecast. This is a directional document: an initial estimate as at 18 September 2026. Nothing on this sheet is built.

SCOPE

One question. Which failure is bought, and what does a byte cost? Placement is sheet 1, shapes are sheet 2.

DIRECTION

Directional as at 2026-09-18. An initial estimate, not a cost forecast.

SEPARATE

A shape that answers one failure boundary does not answer the others (L4-01-018).

REGION vs COUNTRY

Sydney and Melbourne are two AWS Regions in one country. Two regions is not two jurisdictions (L4-01-020).

BIAS

An arbiter in Melbourne biases the vote toward Australia. In T3, Australia then holds 4 of 7 (L4-01-021).

BOTH ENDS

Cross-zone traffic is charged at both ends. That is why a GB across a zone costs $0.02, not $0.01 (L4-01-026).

ONE WAY

Cross-region traffic is charged on the sending side only. Inbound is free, so direction changes the bill (L4-01-027).

LEVER

At these volumes the three-zone spread is cheap. The lever is how much is mirrored, not how many zones are used.

OPEN

Actual GB per day per cell is not known. Every figure here is a shape, not a forecast (L4-01-O04, owned by business and architecture; L4-01-039).
