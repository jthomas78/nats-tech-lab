High-Level Architecture Landscape

How people and organisations enter the logistics SaaS, how Linebooker authorizes and operates it, how NATS and Temporal connect business capabilities to distinct stores, and where the initial South Africa, Australia and Botswana deployment cells run.

Tenant trust and messaging

One NATS account per marketplace tenant. Organisation memberships, RBAC/ABAC and short-lived credentials operate inside that authority. The account boundary is independent of geography.

Regional cells and sovereignty

South Africa and Australia are shared cells with policy-approved gateway subjects. Botswana is isolated: regulated compute, transit, state, telemetry, keys, backups and recovery remain in-country.

Distinct state responsibilities

PostgreSQL is transactional truth; JetStream retains durable facts; Temporal owns workflow state; projections/KV serve rebuildable reads; an adapter selects the regional evidence-document repository.
