Technology Selection and Rationale

Directional · as at 3 September 2026

Directional. First-pass selections for V3, not settled decisions. This view answers which technologies the platform uses and why: the problem each one solves, the features actually relied on, and what was rejected. It deliberately draws no relationships — LB-V3-L2-01 owns how the platform is constructed. The argument for each selection lives in its ADR; where no V3 ADR exists the card says so, and that gap is a to-do rather than a defect.

SCOPE

This is an inventory, not a topology. It answers what and why. LB-V3-L2-01 answers how the parts fit together; the two sit at L2 because they ask different questions.

STATUS

One decision is accepted for V3. Only ADR-053 is V3-scoped. Thirteen of the sixteen cards carry no V3 ADR at all, and two cite a lab-scoped ADR that does not accept the technology for V3.

ADR

The ADR is the record, not this sheet. Each card gives one line of reasoning and a pointer. Do not restate an argument here; it will drift and the diagram will win.

LAB

Proven in lab is not chosen. The Dictionary POC exercised NATS, JetStream, KV, Temporal, Go and the frontend stack at demo scale, under one operator, with no regulated data.

GAP

Read the amber text as a work list. Each "no V3 ADR yet" marks a selection the platform already leans on without a written decision behind it.

STORES

Four stores, four jobs. Postgres is transactional truth, JetStream is durable fact history, KV is a derived read model, and object storage holds evidence documents. They are not interchangeable.

VENDOR

No cloud vendor is selected. S3-compatible names an API contract that several vendors implement. Compute, network and managed-service vendors remain open decisions.

DIVERGE

Two lab decisions do not carry into V3 as written. ADR-048 chose NATS Object Store for lab documents; V3 proposes an S3-compatible repository instead. ADR-051 is scoped to one lab service.

DERIVED

Versions, sizing and configuration are out of scope. Pinned versions, high-availability topology, runbooks, subjects and schemas are L3 and L4 decisions.

REVIEW

A directional document expires. Re-date it whenever a card changes. A position with no date cannot be trusted a year from now.

External Services and Integrations

Directional · as at 3 September 2026

Directional. Third-party services the platform depends on, carried forward from V2 unless stated. Sheet 1 covers technologies Linebooker builds on; this sheet covers services Linebooker buys or integrates with, where the vendor owns the roadmap and the outage. Every card here is inherited operational reality, not a fresh V3 selection — none has been re-tendered, and the final card records what is deliberately not carried forward.

SCOPE

Bought, not built. Sheet 1 lists what the platform is constructed from. This sheet lists services outside the trust and release boundary, where an outage is somebody else's incident.

SOURCE

Every card was read out of the V2 codebase. This is an observed inventory of the running platform, not a wish list. Absence from this sheet means absence from V2.

STATUS

No card here is chosen for V3. Carrying a vendor forward by default is itself a decision; it has simply not been written down yet.

REGION

Four services are South Africa only. Card payments, bank transfer, the voucher retailer and most telematics vendors do not extend to an Australian cell. Each needs a regional equivalent or a deliberate gap.

LOCK-IN

Document extraction is the deepest lock-in. A trained processor is not portable between vendors. Payments, email and messaging are comparatively swappable.

OVERLAP

Two services do the same job. The identity provider and the verification service both offer a second factor. Keeping both is a cost and a support burden.

CONTRACT

Integrate against a contract, never a vendor. Each of these belongs behind an adapter owned by one service, so a vendor change is one adapter, not a migration.

DROPPED

The last card is the honest half. An inventory that lists only what is kept hides the size of the change. What is dropped is a decision that needs a record too.

SECRETS

Every service on this sheet holds a credential. V3 must source them from a secret store; this sheet does not name endpoints, keys or account identifiers.

NEXT

L3-04 External Integration owns the detail. Adapter shape, retry and idempotency rules, and per-vendor contracts belong there, not on this sheet.
