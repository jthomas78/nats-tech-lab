Logical Architecture and Service Model

This view explains how role-aware channels, integration contracts, the control plane, logistics services and platform foundations collaborate. It preserves the L1 business boundaries while exposing service ownership, NATS messaging, Temporal coordination and distinct data responsibilities.

SCOPE

L2 explains construction. Subjects, schemas, workflow steps, permission claims and runtime sizing remain in L3/L4.

AUTH

Authentication and authorization are separate. WorkOS or an enterprise IdP proves identity; Linebooker resolves tenant, organisation, role and attributes.

TENANT

A tenant is a marketplace authority. Its NATS account is a hard messaging boundary; organisations and memberships live inside that authority.

CONTROL

The control plane manages shared authority. It provisions tenants, identities, claims, policy, reference data, entitlements and operational oversight.

SERVICES

Services align to business ownership. Replicas may scale independently, but each service owns its rules, transactional state and published contracts.

NATS

NATS is communication and durable fact history. Core supports live request/reply; JetStream retains selected commands and events.

TEMPORAL

Temporal coordinates long-lived work. Tender expiry, execution, proof, claims and settlement span services without moving domain ownership into workflows.

DATA

Stores are not interchangeable. PostgreSQL is transactional truth by default; JetStream is event history; KV/projections are derived reads; Temporal stores workflow state.

DOCS

Evidence uses an adapter. POD, licences and other large documents go to an S3-compatible repository selected per deployment, not NATS Object Store by default.

AUDIT

Immutable audit is broader than event sourcing. Business facts and security/administrative actions are retained tamper-evidently; event-sourced reconstruction is selective.

Regional Deployment Model

This view applies the logical platform to three initial placement cells. South Africa and Australia may exchange explicitly approved NATS subject classes through gateways; Botswana is a sovereign in-country cell whose regulated business data, transit, operations and recovery do not cross the national boundary.

TOPOLOGY

Start with independent regional clusters. Gateways are an explicit policy exception for shared cells, not an assumed global mesh.

NATS

The trust authority has a home. Operator signing keys and the primary catalogue run in South Africa; each cell uses local resolvers and short-lived credentials.

TENANT

Account and region are separate axes. One tenant account may exist only in its approved cells; organisation roles do not create new NATS accounts.

JS

JetStream is local by default. Streams, KV and selected durable facts remain in their assigned domain unless an approved design adds replication.

BOTSWANA

The sovereign boundary includes transit. Compute, NATS traffic, workflow state, databases, documents, telemetry, keys, backups and recovery stay in Botswana.

CONTROL

Botswana requires a local control-plane slice. Only signed policy, claims and non-sensitive deployment metadata may enter, subject to legal approval.

SERVICES

Cells repeat the same logical service model. Web assets, the MFE registry, replicas, workers and adapters are served inside the placement boundary.

TEMPORAL

Workflow hosting follows placement. The runtime, workers and persistence are cell-resident; Botswana is self-hosted in-country.

STORAGE

Every stateful technology follows placement. Service databases and evidence repositories are independently backed up and recovered inside the permitted geography.

SECURITY

Controls apply at every ingress and hop. TLS, encryption at rest, RBAC/ABAC, short-lived credentials, masking and immutable audit are cell-local obligations.

VALIDATE

This is a proposed compliance posture. Botswana legal, cloud availability, network, support and recovery constraints still require formal validation.
