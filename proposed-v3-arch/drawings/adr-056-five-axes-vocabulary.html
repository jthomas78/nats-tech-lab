ADR-056 . PROPOSED LINEBOOKER V3 . PLATFORM VOCABULARY

Five Independent Axes

Region, tenant, account, cell and JetStream domain each answer a different question. No two of them may be defined as permanently equal. Companion figure to ADR-056; it is not a catalogued LB-V3 document and carries no LB-V3 identifier.

Five questions, five words

The axes cross, they do not nest

One word, three meanings

What this does and does not settle

  • Settled -- the words. Each of the five answers one question, and the mapping between any two is a choice recorded per tenant, never a law.
  • Settled -- the naming. A bare “domain” means JetStream. The DNS sense is a market host. The DDD sense is a business domain, written in full.
  • Not settled -- the mappings. How many accounts a tenant owns, how many cells a region holds, and whether a service replica is shared across accounts all stay open as L3-01-O01 to L3-01-O05, L3-01-O07 and L3-01-O08.
  • Not settled -- the sweep. Existing material saying “site domain” is now wrong and must be renamed.
