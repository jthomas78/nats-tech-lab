Linebooker Logistics SaaS - System Context

Scope: the proposed Linebooker Logistics SaaS is the one software system in scope. Audience: business stakeholders, product teams, architects and new engineers. Internal applications, services, stores, NATS topology and regional deployment are deliberately deferred.

Marketplace participation

Shippers create demand; transporters supply capacity; drivers execute work. Organisation membership provides the business context for every user action.

Linebooker responsibilities

Linebooker operates the logistics business. Linebooker Tech provides and runs the multi-tenant SaaS platform supporting that business.

Trust and sovereignty

External identity proves who a user is. Linebooker decides what they may do and applies country policy to regulated processing, transit and storage.
