Streams, aggregates and CQRS — where each one sits

An aggregate is one business thing with rules; a stream is a JetStream storage log; a subject joins them.

In one line each

  • Aggregate: one business thing with an ID and rules (truck-1).
  • Aggregate type: the kind of thing (vehicle).
  • Stream: JetStream log with name, subjects and limits (ODOMETER).
  • Subject: the label that ties one aggregate to its events (evt.odometer.vehicle.truck-1.>).
  • Rule: streams per domain = 1 or one per aggregate type. Never one per aggregate.
