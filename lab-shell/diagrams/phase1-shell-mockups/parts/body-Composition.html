Reference data two ways: NATS KV as the read model vs. KV as a cache in front of a Postgres CQRS projection.

Intro — demos/01-dictionary/README.md

The POC evaluated three side-by-side CQRS shapes over a shipping domain with Ship and Container aggregates before settling on one: canonical projection in Postgres, with NATS KV as an eager write-through cache in front of it.

The same JetStream event handler that upserts Postgres overwrites the KV entry; a cache miss falls through to Postgres. Shapes A and C were retired once the comparison was decided.

Stream SHIPPING uses LimitsPolicy, not InterestPolicy — replay is what the write side rehydrates from.
