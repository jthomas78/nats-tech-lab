What actually differs

Both — one Vue app, WebSocket on the demo's NATS, KV watch on both buckets, a stream tail, the lag lane, and the snapshot measurement.
Only B — a Go HTTP shim (cqrs serve), a second host port, CORS, and a change to the demo's "no services" rule.
Neither — business rules in JavaScript. In both mockups every accept and every refusal comes from domain.go.

One open question either way: demo 01 already claims 7100–7149 for its za-1 cell and 7150–7199 for au-1, so the 7100 band has no room left for a demo 04 frontend. We either carve a slice out of the band or give demo 04 a port outside it, the way NUI took 31311.
