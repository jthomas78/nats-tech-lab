Multi-cluster topologies and accounts

Can the JetStream meta leader be kept in the hub? What happens to metadata and to regional data when a region goes dark, and when it comes back?

Three clusters of three servers, joined by gateways: za, au and the hub, arb. Nine servers vote on metadata; five make a quorum. This demo is for validation only. The replay shows what the rig recorded. It does not run NATS, and it does not predict what NATS would do.

How to read the replay

Observed
A reading a server gave, with its time. Values change only when a new reading arrives.
Recorded result
A check, note or verdict row, with its ID (for example ML54).
Derived
Quorum arithmetic only: answering voters against the quorum of five.
No reading
The rig recorded nothing here. Nothing is filled in.
