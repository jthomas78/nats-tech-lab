Regional accounts: what they split, and what they don't

An account per region gives each region its own stream namespace. It does not give it its own JetStream vote. Setup: a single three-server AU cluster grows to T4 (AU + ZA + a 3-node arbiter site, joined by gateways, one meta group of 9, majority 5) or to T5 (hub + leaf cluster per region, three meta groups). Note: the lab's T1 is two unlinked clusters, a different starting point.

In one line each

  • Accounts split names. Different accounts can each own ORDERS; within one T4 account, a stream name is taken supercluster-wide.
  • Prefixes are for people. LB_AU_ and LB_ZA_ express ownership, with no placement or availability semantics.
  • Placement is explicit. Set "placement": {"cluster": "au"} to choose AU; without placement, JetStream prefers the client's cluster, subject to resources and constraints.
  • The vote is shared. T4 accounts share 9 meta voters and a majority of 5. T5 has three independent meta groups, each needing its local quorum.
  • DR is still your design. Keep regional dependencies available and plan copies, promotion, consumer continuity and client failover; neither topology supplies that plan.
