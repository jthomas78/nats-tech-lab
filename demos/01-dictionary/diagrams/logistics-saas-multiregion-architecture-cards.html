Architecture reference · logistics SaaS · NATS

A Multi-region Logistics SaaS
on NATS

How shippers and transporters meet through a governed marketplace; how NATS accounts isolate tenants; and how the same service and data model deploys across regions and logistics edge locations.

The governing invariant

Region is placement. Tenant is authority. Organisation is participation. A marketplace tenant may span regions and contain many shippers and transporters, but every interaction is mediated by platform services and constrained by account, membership, role, and subject permissions.

What the public Linebooker model adds

The business loop is not merely "customer talks to transporter." It is procure, aggregate, execute and settle: fixed-lane tenders, bidding loads, truck commitment, route execution, live tracking, digital POD, claims and consolidated payment.

Logistics SaaS architectureCard 01 · System landscape
01
Region is placement · account is authority

One SaaS, many regions, hard tenant walls

A regional NATS supercluster carries the live logistics fabric. Human identity enters through WorkOS; signed NATS credentials place every browser and service inside exactly one account boundary.

Geography

Clusters provide regional HA. Gateways make the clusters one supercluster. A tenant can operate in several regions without region becoming part of its identity.

Identity + tenancy

WorkOS identifies the human; NATS authorizes the connection. The minted credential binds a user or service to one account and a least-privilege subject set.

Data placement

Persistence stays regional by policy. Postgres is authoritative; JetStream domains retain local event history. Mirror only the streams approved for DR or global views.

Subject taxonomy

Four subject families carry every interaction. evt.{context}.{service}.{entity}.{entity-id}.{event} for replayable domain facts, rpc.* between services, api.* from a browser and notify.* for live change. {context} is the business unit — never the tenant and never the region, because tenancy is the account wall and region is a deployment choice.

Card 01

The topology scales in two independent directions: add a region without redefining tenants, or add a tenant without creating a new regional messaging fabric.

02
The marketplace is the conversation

How shippers and transporters engage

They never receive a direct technical channel to one another. Each side acts through a role-specific client; marketplace services validate, rank, allocate, notify and preserve the audit trail.

Fixed contract lanes

Longer-term tenders establish lane rates, committed volumes and selected transporter capacity.

Daily bidding loads

Spot opportunities absorb excess fleet and return legs without bypassing vetting or allocation rules.

Execution closes the loop

Tracking, exceptions, digital POD, claims and settlement remain part of the same governed load record.

Vetting gate

Capacity is admitted before it can compete. A transporter is registered, vetted and onboarded before any tender or bidding subject is granted, so allocation draws only from approved carriers and agreed truck types.

Logistics SaaS architectureCard 05 · Authentication
05
Identity is not messaging authority

From human sign-in to a NATS session

WorkOS establishes who the person is. Accounts-service decides which active membership they may use, then mints a short-lived credential whose signing key constrains the effective NATS permissions.

Authentication

WorkOS handles password, enterprise SSO, MFA and directory-driven lifecycle.

Authorisation

NATS validates the operator-account-user trust chain and enforces subject permissions server-side.

HTTP exception

Where direct HTTP is unavoidable, first mint a short-lived, single-use capability over the authenticated NATS connection.

Renewal rule

A session ends when its credential ends. The browser renews over the connection it already holds, and a revoked membership simply stops the next renewal — so withdrawing access never depends on a client honouring a logout.

Logistics SaaS architectureCard 06 · Deployment model
06
Repeatable regional cells

Deploy regions, not bespoke tenant stacks

Each region is a repeatable cell: a three-node NATS cluster, the same versioned service set, service-owned persistence and regional ingress. Gateways connect cells without turning the WAN into one cluster.

Pooled default
  • One service deployment per region serves many tenant accounts
  • Each active tenant gets its own NATS connection and data scope
  • Best operational efficiency for the standard tier
Optional silo tier
  • Dedicate selected service replicas or storage to a tenant
  • Keep the same subjects, contracts and onboarding control plane
  • Use for regulation, scale or noisy-neighbour isolation
ConcernPer regional cellGlobal to the platform
NATS routingA cluster of three nodes across availability zonesGateways join the clusters into one supercluster
JetStreamA named domain holding that region's streamsOnly approved mirrors may leave the region
PostgresService-owned authority plus regional backupsNo global write master

ReleaseThe same versioned service set, deployed locallyOne catalogue of service and UI versions

07
Truth, history and fast views are different jobs

Each service owns its facts

Postgres is authoritative for transactional state. JetStream retains replayable domain facts. KV and projections make current state fast to read; Object Store holds POD and compliance documents.

DomainAuthoritative recordsDurable events / objectsFast views and consumers
MarketplaceTender, lane, offer, award, allocationPublished, bid submitted, awarded, allocatedOpportunity board, ranking, award audit
FleetTruck, trailer, driver, eligibility, availabilityVetted, availability changed, committedEligible-capacity index, transporter fleet view

TrackingProvider link, latest fix, consent and retentionNormalised position and exception eventsLive map, ETA projection, corridor analytics

Event-source when

History is itself a business concern: replaying a trip, auditing an award, reconstructing allocation or proving a payment transition. Use CRUD when only current lookup state matters.

Postgres — truth

Commits the decision in one transaction and answers what is contractually true right now.

JetStream — history

Retains the fact under a limits policy, so a trip, award or payment sequence can be replayed later.

KV + Object Store

KV is an eager write-through cache for current views; Object Store holds POD blobs whose metadata stays in Postgres.

Card 07

Logistics SaaS architectureCard 08 · Tracking and edge
08
Many telemetry shapes, one trip timeline

Tracking becomes a normalised business signal

Vehicle devices, transporter systems and driver apps differ. Provider adapters normalise their inputs before trip services derive milestones, ETA and exceptions for both client experiences.

Privacy boundary

Share only tracking for an allocated load and its permitted time window. A shipper never gains general access to a transporter's fleet.

Quality boundary

Reject stale, impossible or duplicate fixes; retain provider provenance so ETA and dispute decisions remain explainable.

Adapter rule

A raw provider payload is never the domain contract. If a provider changes its format, only its adapter changes; trip services, client projections and the settlement gate keep reading the same normalised events.

Card 08

Tracking is not a separate dashboard feed; it is evidence attached to the same allocated trip and visible through each role's authorised projection.

Logistics SaaS architectureCard 09 · Resilience
09
Availability does not erase residency

Fail locally before failing across regions

A node or service failure should be invisible inside its regional cell. A regional evacuation is a policy-driven event: traffic may move quickly, but authoritative data moves only through an approved DR design.

FailureExpected responseWhat must not happen
One NATS node / AZClients reconnect inside the cluster; JetStream leader changesCross-region failover for a local node loss
Service replicaQueue subscriber or load balancer selects a healthy replicaDuplicate non-idempotent command execution
WAN / gatewayRegions and leaf sites continue locally; replay after reconnectionAssume a disconnected edge is permanently failed
External GPS / WorkOSDegrade the affected capability and surface stale/blocked stateLose active trip state or widen access to compensate
Whole regionApply tenant-specific routing, data-promotion and residency runbookPromote unapproved or incomplete data globally
Decide contractually

Home region, permitted recovery regions, RTO, RPO, POD residency and whether active trips may fail across borders.

Test operationally

Node loss, account suspension, expired credentials, gateway partition, stale telemetry, projector replay and database promotion.

Blast radius

Contain a failure at the smallest boundary that can absorb it: node, then replica, then cell, then region.

Evidence

Every failover records what moved, when it moved and which tenant policy permitted it.

Re-entry

Returning to the home region is a planned operation with the same approval gates as leaving it.

Card 09

A supercluster provides reachability; it does not by itself define database recovery, data sovereignty or business continuity.

Logistics SaaS architectureCard 10 · Provisioning and operations
10
Two repeatable control-plane workflows

Add a tenant; clone a region

Neither operation is a ticket-driven collection of manual NATS edits. The control plane applies versioned policy, records every step and exposes readiness only when identity, messaging, data and UI agree.

Desired state

Tenant, account, imports, signing keys, regions, service release and UI catalogue are versioned configuration.

Observed state

Connections, claims, consumers, stream health, projector lag, database readiness and plugin health are measured independently.

Activation gate

Do not mark a tenant or region ready because provisioning returned 200. Require end-to-end probes from identity through a business read.

Readiness probeWhat it provesBlocks activation when
Identity round tripA test member signs in and receives a scoped credentialSign-in, membership or signing key is wrong
Account isolationA cross-account publish is refused by the serverImports or exports are wider than intended
Stream and consumerA published fact is stored and then consumedDomain, retention or consumer is missing
Projection readA business read returns the projected factProjector lag or a migration is incomplete
UI catalogueThe shell loads the role-filtered contributionsA plugin manifest or version is unavailable
Rollback rule

Every provisioning step has a reverse step. Suspend an account, rotate or revoke a signing key, retire a release or withdraw a UI contribution — without deleting the tenant's history.

Card 10

Provisioning is a reconciled workflow with an audit trail; readiness is an observed property, not an optimistic flag.

Evidence and design status

What is sourced, settled and proposed

This document combines the repository's implemented NATS patterns, public statements about Linebooker's operating model and explicit architecture proposals for a future multi-region logistics SaaS.

Repository-grounded
  • NATS operator mode with PLATFORM, tenant and system account boundaries
  • Accounts-service mints short-lived credentials; auth-service is merged into it
  • Per-tenant service connections; context-scoped subjects and KV keys
  • Postgres projection as truth with JetStream history and eager KV cache
  • WorkOS user lifecycle is documented, with implementation status called out separately
Linebooker public model
  • Marketplace connects shippers/customers with vetted transport companies and trucks
  • Fixed contract lane rates dominate; bidding supports surplus capacity and return legs
  • Procure, aggregate and execute cover tendering, allocation, tracking, POD and payment
  • GPS-integrated tracking, digital POD, control-tower visibility and consolidated settlement
  • Transporters register, are vetted, receive opportunities, submit/accept, execute and get paid
Architecture proposals requiring design approval
  • Johannesburg and Cape Town shown as example regional cells; actual cloud regions and residency classes remain to be selected
  • Pooled regional services are the default, with an optional silo tier for regulation, scale or noisy-neighbour isolation
  • Tenant means marketplace-operating business; shipper and transporter organisations participate inside it through membership-scoped grants
  • Selected JetStream mirrors, Postgres recovery and Object Store replication are governed separately; no automatic global data movement is assumed
  • Domain boxes are bounded contexts; final service boundaries, event schemas, RTO/RPO and retention policies need business-rule approval
