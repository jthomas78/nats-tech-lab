Linebooker Logistics SaaS - Platform and Container Overview

Scope: separately runnable applications, services, messaging, workflow and stores inside the proposed Linebooker system. Audience: architects, engineering leads, security and operations. Regions, instance counts and country placement belong in the separate Deployment view.

Trust is resolved before business work

External identity proof is converted into Linebooker tenant, organisation, membership and attribute context before a business service accepts a command.

Messaging has explicit roles

NATS Core carries request/reply and selected live subjects. JetStream retains durable domain facts. Temporal owns workflow progress and retry state.

State remains deliberately distinct

PostgreSQL is operational truth, projections and KV are rebuildable reads, the evidence repository stores documents, and audit storage follows masking and residency policy.
