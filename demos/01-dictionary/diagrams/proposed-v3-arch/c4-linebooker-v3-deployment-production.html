Multi-Region and Multi-Tenant Deployment

Scope: logical tenant-account placement and production-shaped runtime cells for South Africa, Australia and Botswana. Audience: architecture, platform engineering, security and operations. Tenant is a marketplace security boundary; region is a deployment and residency boundary.

Shared-cell topology

South Africa and Australia run independent clusters and resident stores. Only explicitly approved NATS accounts and subject families may traverse their gateway.

Trust authority placement

The primary NATS operator signing hierarchy is controlled in the South Africa control plane. Other cells use local claim resolvers and short-lived credentials.

Botswana sovereignty

Processing, transit, messages, databases, documents, telemetry, keys, backups and recovery for regulated workloads remain within Botswana.
