V3 target architecture · system design §1.3 / DD-04a

Three-plane, multi-region logistics platform

A global SaaS control plane governs repeatable regional cells. Each tenant is a marketplace-operating business with a server-enforced NATS account boundary; its shippers, transporters and users work in the tenant product plane.

Decisions made visible

  • Subscription billing is control-plane work. SaaS plans, entitlements, metering and invoices to tenants are cross-tenant concerns. Freight payments and shipper/transporter settlement remain product-plane concerns.
  • “Operator” is reserved for the NATS trust root. Human-facing administration is named platform administration or marketplace operations to avoid an overloaded term.
  • Shared platform capabilities remain separate services. Accounts/Auth, RefData/Config and Observability are not collapsed into one high-privilege “platform” service.
  • Multi-region data movement is still a design decision. Residency, replication and disaster-recovery policy must be agreed before adding cross-region NATS federation.
