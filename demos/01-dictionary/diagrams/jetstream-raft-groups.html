JetStream clustering · RAFT replication model

Three RAFT group types, each with a different scope

RAFT is not one cluster-wide log for all JetStream data. A JetStream cluster has one metadata group, then separate groups for every stream and consumer that needs replicated state.

What differs in practice

  • Meta group: one per JetStream cluster; records the cluster’s JetStream configuration and placement decisions. It is the control plane.
  • Stream group: one per stream; replicates retained messages and stream state according to that stream’s replica count. It is the data log.
  • Consumer group: one per consumer; replicates consumer progress such as acknowledgements, pending messages, and delivery attempts when the consumer is replicated. It is delivery state, not another copy of the stream.
