What changes, and the two calls worth confirming

  • Markup. The <button class="sidebar-collapse-btn"> moves from the last child of .sidebar to a new <div class="sidebar-head"> that is the first child, above .nav-scroll. No change to toggleCollapsed(), the collapsed ref, or any of the four apps' #sidebar slot content.
  • Vertical alignment. The rail's padding-top: 14px plus a 26px header row puts the icon's centre at y≈27px below the topbar — it does not line up with the theme toggle in the topbar (those are separated by the topbar's own bottom border). Matching the screenshot means top of the rail, not top of the window.
  • Glyph. The panel-toggle icon replaces the « text chevron, and the collapsed state mirrors the icon rather than reusing transform: scaleX(-1). If you would rather keep the chevron and only change its position, that is a one-line variant of state B — say so and the icon swap is dropped.
  • Accessibility. The button gains an aria-label (Collapse sidebar / Expand sidebar) and aria-expanded; today it has neither, and a bare « reads as "left-pointing double angle quotation mark" to a screen reader.
