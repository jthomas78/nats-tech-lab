Tracing — before and after the OpenTelemetry move

Before Phase 54 a hand-rolled tracer published spans over NATS and two consumers read them. Now the OTel SDK exports straight to a Collector, Tempo stores the traces, and clients query them over NATS api.* through observability-service — never Tempo directly.

What changed

  • Removed (54d): shared/natstrace, the obs.trace.* envelope, the TRACES stream, the trace-request-reply KV bucket and its projector, otlp-bridge, Jaeger, the tenant obs.trace.> export/import, the Admin UI KV-watch feed (useTraceFeed.js), cmd/seed-traces, and GET /api/nats/services.
  • Added: OTel SDK in each service through one package, shared/otelnats; OTel Collector; Tempo; Grafana (login required); NATS api._platform.observability.trace.{search|get|services}.v1 on observability-service for operator clients; api._platform.observability.service.list.v1 for the Services panel (BR-072).
  • Kept: request/reply bodies (as opt-in span events, redact before truncate), one span per logical call (BR-037), durable buffering (moved from the TRACES stream to the Collector's disk queue), the obs.pubsub.* wire tap (ARCHITECTURE-OBSERVABILITY, local ADR-047 — not the global ADR-047) untouched.
  • Lost, on purpose: replaying old spans to a new backend; trace history from before the switch (not migrated, decision 16); live push in the Traces panel (now polling — live notifications deferred to proposed Phase 56); the Pulse view, unavailable until Phase 55 (BR-077).
  • Still open: anyone who can reach adminConnectInfo can obtain an operator credential — unchanged by this phase; operator login is candidate Phase 109. The Temporal hop is not traced (accepted limitation).
