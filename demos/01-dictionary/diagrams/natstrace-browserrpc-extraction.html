ARCHITECTURE-COMMUNICATIONS § 6 · Phase 35 (implemented 2026-08-18)

natstrace and browserrpc — two more packages, one shared source each

natstrace wraps every rpc.*/api.* call (or HTTP request, for accounts-service) in an OTLP-shaped span and pushes it onto the KV-backed trace store the Admin UI's Request/Reply panel reads. browserrpc is the reply-tail every browser-facing micro.Request handler needs: read {context} off the subject, stamp the Nats-Responder/Nats-Service-Error headers, and shape success/error JSON the same way everywhere. (The third extracted package, natstenants — the per-tenant connection manager — has its own diagram in ARCHITECTURE-ACCOUNTS.md.)

Same phase, same blocker as natstenants

  • One module strategy, three packages. natstrace and browserrpc were bundled into Phase 35 alongside natstenants because all three hit the identical Go-module/Dockerfile-build-context blocker — once go.work plus the per-service replace directive existed for one package, extracting the other two was the same mechanical move repeated.
  • accounts-service is the one asymmetry in both packages. It consumes natstrace through a different entry point (HTTPMiddleware, since its transport is REST) and doesn't consume browserrpc at all (no browser-facing RPC surface to reply from) — the diagram's dashed box marks that as a deliberate non-consumer, not a missed migration.
  • Verified live. A full docker compose down -v && up --build ran with zero panics, fatals, or auth violations across any container; the Admin UI's Request/Reply trace panel showed 45 live api.*/rpc.* traces at 0 errors, through both refactored adapters end to end.
