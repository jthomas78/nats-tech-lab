Proposed Linebooker V3 Architecture · LB-V3-L1-01 · Level 1

System and Platform Overview

This stakeholder view distinguishes the Linebooker product business from Linebooker Tech, shows internal departments and marketplace participants, identifies governed integration and reporting channels, and explains how country policy shapes placement. Service internals, NATS accounts, workflows and deployment mechanics remain for L2 and derived L3 views.

LINEBOOKER

Linebooker is the product business. Finance includes Creditors and Debtors; Controllers perform logistics control and follow-up for the marketplace operation.

TECH

Linebooker Tech is the technology arm. Its platform administrators and SRE capability provide, operate and support the logistics SaaS.

TENANCY

The tenant is the marketplace-operating business. It is the authority boundary and may span more than one region.

ORGS

Organisations participate inside a tenant. Shippers publish demand; transporters offer capacity and execute; users act through memberships.

EXTERNAL

External systems are ecosystem actors, not business participants. WorkOS, ERP, telematics, maps, payments and notifications connect through contracts.

CHANNELS

One product exposes several governed channels. Role-aware web/mobile experiences sit beside APIs for integration and authorised reporting extracts, never direct database access.

LOGISTICS

The platform owns the governed lifecycle. Tender, award, allocation, trip, tracking, evidence, claims and settlement are mediated rather than direct organisation-to-organisation messaging.

FINANCE

Commercial finance is a first-class domain. Pricing, tax, fees, invoices, payments and transporter settlement follow the marketplace legal entity and contract.

SECURITY

Security and residency are explicit guarantees. Tenant separation, RBAC/ABAC, encryption, masked logs, immutable audit and country policy apply across the platform; Botswana-regulated data stays local.

LEVELS

L1 is the navigation map. L2 will expand application, integration, control, domain, messaging, workflow, data and regional structures without changing these business boundaries.
