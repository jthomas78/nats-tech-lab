What to take from Chronicle

  • Copy the package shape. One Setup() builds traces, logs and metrics and returns one shutdown function; the caller gives the service name. That is decision 14's shared/otelnats, and Phase 55 can reuse the logs half.
  • A shared helper is not a joined trace. Chronicle had Inject and never called it. Only an end-to-end parent/child test catches that — 54a's BR-064 check.
  • Do not copy the tenant model. A per-deployment tenant fits siloed services only. The lab needs a per-span tenant (BR-069).
  • Chronicle has no tests for tracing. No outage test, no access test, no trace assertion.
