V3 tracing with the OpenTelemetry SDK

Every service traces through one shared module, sends OTLP to a disk-buffered agent, never over NATS, and the Grafana stack stores and shows it.

What every span carries

  • Tenant: from trusted context only (rule 6).
  • Location: service resource + cloud.region, NATS server/cluster/address on messaging spans, target JetStream domain on JetStream spans (rule 7).
  • Bodies: off by default, redacted then truncated when on (rule 8).
