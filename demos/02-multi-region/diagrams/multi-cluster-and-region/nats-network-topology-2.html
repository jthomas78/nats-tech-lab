One super-cluster — hub, za, au — and which Compose file creates each part

Revision 1 drew the right shape with the wrong names. The regions are South Africa and Australia — za and au, recorded on 2026-08-11 — not eu-west and us-east. This revision also draws every server as a circle rather than a box, so a cluster reads as a mesh of peers, and adds the figure that was missing: which Compose file creates which part of the topology. NATS joins servers in exactly three ways and no others: routes inside one cluster, gateways between clusters, leafnode connections from the edge inward.

Does the global layer get a cluster? Yes — and here is why

Nothing forces it. The provisioner could just be a client of a region's cluster. Four reasons say give it its own three servers anyway.

  • Blast radius. A runaway tenant in one region cannot exhaust the servers that mint accounts. That is the whole point of a control plane being a separate plane rather than a separate folder.
  • Lifecycle. You upgrade and restart the hub's servers without touching a region, and a region without touching the hub. Shared servers mean a shared maintenance window.
  • It needs R3 of its own. The placement registry — which region a tenant lives in — has to survive a node loss. Three servers give it a real JetStream R3 without borrowing a region's storage or making one region special.
  • No region is the capital. If the provisioner lived in za, then za is structurally more important than au forever, and every later conversation has to work around it. This matters more with two regions than with five, because with two there is nowhere to hide the asymmetry.

And the matching non-reason, which is the part that surprises people:

  • Regions do not ask the hub to log a user in. With resolver: full, every server keeps its own copy of the account JWTs on disk in dir — that is already how demos/01-dictionary/nats/nats.conf is written. A claims update is pushed over the system account when it changes; it is not fetched per connection. So the hub being unreachable stops new tenants being created and does not stop existing ones working. Across a 270 ms link, this is the difference between a working platform and an unusable one.
  • Start it inside the first region if you want to. Three extra servers on day one is a fair thing to defer, and hosting the hub in za is the sensible first step. What must never happen is two of it — there is one operator NKey, and a second minting authority is a permanent fork of your trust chain, not a scaling step.

The three ways NATS joins servers, and what each one costs

LinkJoinsCarriesConstraint that decides it
Route 6222Servers into one cluster, full meshEverything, plus Raft for JetStreamNeeds low latency — so one region, and never stretched
Gateway 7222Cluster to cluster, full mesh of clustersOnly subjects with interest on the far sideWAN-tolerant; carries no stream replicas
Leafnode 7422An edge server to a hub, leaf dials outInterest both ways, over one outbound connectionIts own account and security domain; can hold its own JetStream domain
  • Three servers is not three copies of your data. Servers are a cluster property; replicas are a per-stream property. R3 has to be set on every stream and every KV or Object Store bucket that matters, and right now nothing sets it.
  • One super-cluster is one JetStream system. A stream is pinned to a cluster by placement, and its replicas stay inside that cluster. Getting the same data into two regions is a mirror or a source — named, explicit, and eventually consistent.
  • The gap between the drawing and the repo is one server. nats.conf today is server_name: nats-dev-1 with a client port, a monitor port, a WebSocket port, JetStream, and the operator plus resolver: full. No cluster, no gateway, no leafnodes. Every line in this sketch is additive to that file.
  • Botswana is not on this drawing, and that is deliberate. A sovereign in-country cell needs its own operator, its own resolver and no gateway path at all — per L2-020 it is a second trust domain, not a third cluster in this mesh. There is also no AWS region in Botswana, so it cannot be drawn as af-south-1 either. That conflict is still open and is not this figure's job to settle.
