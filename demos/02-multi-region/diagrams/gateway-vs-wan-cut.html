Gateway or no gateway — stream collisions and WAN cuts

Two questions, asked of three topologies. Do two regions fight over one stream name? And what happens when the link between them dies? The account fixes the first. Nothing here fixes the second.

What to take away

  • The account fixes the collision. It does not fix the WAN. Those are two different problems and only one of them has a fix in this lab.
  • “Freeze” means management only. Existing streams keep reading and writing through a cut, because a stream’s copies all live inside one cluster. What stops is creating, deleting and editing streams and consumers. Plan a WAN cut as a change freeze, not an outage.
  • Uneven regions do not both freeze. Majority is counted over the whole meta group. 3 + 3 splits 3 / 3 and nobody wins. 3 + 2 splits so the side with 3 of 5 keeps its leader and stays fully writable; only the smaller side freezes.
  • A gateway buys reach and costs a shared failure domain. Six servers across two regions is one JetStream meta group, so the link between them is a hard dependency for all stream management.
  • The usual fix is a third cluster — a gateway hub. With three sites a majority survives losing one link. This lab has no hub; docs/Multi-Region-Plan.md names one.
  • A JetStream domain is not on this list, on purpose. A domain names a JetStream system and must be identical on every server of a supercluster. Per-region domains were tried here and silently broke JetStream. See nats/nats.conf.
